What services does Domireek provide?+

We provide Odoo ERP implementation and customization, custom software, web and mobile applications, API integrations, AI automation, cloud deployment and ongoing technical support.

Can you customize Odoo for our workflows?+

Yes. We can configure standard Odoo features and develop focused custom modules, reports, approvals and automations where the standard system does not meet your requirements.

Can you migrate data from another ERP?+

Yes. We plan, clean, map, import and validate data from legacy ERPs, spreadsheets or older Odoo versions to reduce risk and disruption.

Do you provide support after launch?+

Yes. We offer ongoing troubleshooting, monitoring, performance improvements, user support and new feature development after go-live.

Can you build solutions outside Odoo?+

Yes. Odoo is our main ERP specialization, but we also develop custom web platforms, mobile apps, APIs, AI solutions and cloud infrastructure as standalone products or connected to Odoo.
